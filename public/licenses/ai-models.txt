On-device models and runtimes used by OpenAnyDoc's OCR PDF, Translate PDF, Blur faces, Remove background and Upscale image tools. Each is a file of
this site, read by the browser on first use; pictures and documents never leave the device.

Runtimes
- tesseract.js 7.0.0 and tesseract.js-core 6.1.2 (OCR PDF). Apache License 2.0. https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js
- onnxruntime-web 1.31.1 (the image tools). MIT License, Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation. https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime
- @browsermt/bergamot-translator 0.4.9 (Translate PDF; translator.js and translate/runtime/, unmodified except the worker's address).
  Mozilla Public License 2.0. Source: https://github.com/browsermt/bergamot-translator

Models (in /models/)
- tessdata/eng.traineddata, tessdata/ara.traineddata: tessdata_fast. Apache License 2.0. https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast
- ultraface-rfb-320.onnx: Ultra-Light-Fast-Generic-Face-Detector-1MB (version RFB-320, from the ONNX Model Zoo). MIT License,
  Copyright (c) 2019 linzai. https://github.com/Linzaer/Ultra-Light-Fast-Generic-Face-Detector-1MB
- u2netp.onnx: U²-Net (small), as distributed by rembg. Apache License 2.0. https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net
- modnet-quantized.onnx: MODNet, ONNX conversion by Xenova. Apache License 2.0. https://github.com/ZHKKKe/MODNet
- swin2sr-lightweight-x2-quantized.onnx: Swin2SR lightweight x2, ONNX conversion by Xenova of caidas/swin2SR-lightweight-x2-64.
  Apache License 2.0. https://github.com/mv-lab/swin2sr
- translate/enar/, translate/aren/: Firefox Translations models, base-memory, English to Arabic and Arabic to English (model,
  lexical shortlist and vocabulary, gzipped). Mozilla Public License 2.0. https://github.com/mozilla/firefox-translations-models
